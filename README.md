TileSnake.io – Multiplayer Territory Battle

TileSnake.io is an exciting, fast-paced multiplayer territory-control game where players compete to conquer the arena, expand their territory, and dominate the leaderboard!

Take control of your neon-coloured snake and venture beyond your territory to create trails. Loop back safely to claim new land and grow your empire. But watch out! Your trail is vulnerable while you're outside your territory. Other players can cut your trail to eliminate you, so stay alert, plan your moves, and strike at the perfect moment!

Game Features
Real-Time Multiplayer: Battle against players from around the world.
Territory Conquest: Expand your territory by drawing loops and capturing land.
Competitive Combat: Cut enemy trails and eliminate your opponents.
Speed Boosts: Collect power-ups to move faster and gain an advantage.
Shields: Protect yourself with special defensive power-ups.
Tower Capture: Surround powerful towers with your territory to claim them and trigger massive area expansions.
Overdrive: Earn kills to unlock powerful Overdrive abilities.
Live Leaderboard: Compete for the biggest territory and the most eliminations.
Play With Friends: Create multiplayer rooms and invite friends using shareable links.
Customizable Settings: Adjust your UI size, minimap, kill feed, and leaderboard.
How to Play
Enter your name and jump into the arena.
Leave your territory to draw a trail.
Return safely to your territory to capture new land.
Attack opponents by crossing their trails.
Avoid enemy attacks and protect your own trail.
Collect power-ups and capture towers.
Grow your territory, eliminate rivals, and climb the leaderboard!
