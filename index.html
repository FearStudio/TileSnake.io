HOW TO PLAY

Controls

  • WASD or Arrow keys: steer in four directions.
  • Swipe: swipe on the screen to steer on mobile.
  • Space, tap, or the opposite direction: stop your snake. From a stop, turn in any direction except directly back onto your trail.

Claim territory

Leave your coloured territory, draw a loop, and return to your own land to claim the enclosed area. Your exposed trail is vulnerable—cross an opponent's trail to cut them, but avoid trails yourself.

Beacons / Towers

Surround a beacon (tower) with your colour to capture it. Capturing a tower claims/floods territory around it. Captured towers can also fire their colour outward; watch for their recharge and firing rings.

Random perks

  • » Speed: boosts your movement speed temporarily.
  • ◈ Shield: gives you protection temporarily.

Perks spawn around the map—move over one to collect it. Use boosts wisely while making risky territory runs.
